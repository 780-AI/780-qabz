# 054_mci_mobile_payment — MCI mobile bill order and payment

Status: done (2026-11-16; the operator QA on stage waits for the operator)

## Description

Pay an MCI mobile bill. Read tasks 044 (landline), 045 (status worker) and 048 (water) and `docs/this_vs_bill.md` items 38 to 47 first: the order flow (`core/order`, `billTypes`), the payment step (`order.Payments`, `PayRoutes` in `src/app/defaults.go`), the Parsian adapter `Pay` and the shared status worker already exist. The design step reads `../bill` (commit `ff98e25`) again and lists the steps for the operator before any code, as task 048 did.

**What `bill` does (read 2026-11-16):** `Phone.NewOrderV2` (`controller/phone/newOrder.go`) maps the operator and the term to a bill type: mci `fullTerm` to `mobile`, mci `midTerm` to `mobilemidterm`. The V2 token `data` keys are `type`, `phone`, `service` (the operator), `billId`, `payId`, `payAmount`, `rawAmount`, `about`, `aboutIcon`, `receiptMobile`, `invoiceMobile` (the phone in the 0 form) and, with a fee, `feeAmount` and `feeDescription`; task 039 already builds this for Irancell. The payment gateway (`gateway/payment/mobile_mci.go`) has two providers: `JiringSwitchMCIMobile` (`BillPay` of the `jiring-switch` service; `provider_order_id` = day of the year and the RRN) and `ParsianMCIMobile` (`TopBill/Pay`, the inquiry type of the request). The "bill already paid" window is `parsian.settlementInquiryMCIMobile` (Jiring Switch: 0). No settlement. Seed order providers: row 30 `jiring-switch` for `mobile` and row 29 for `mobilemidterm` (both disabled), row 48 `parsian` for `mobile` (disabled), row 149 `parsian` for `mobilemidterm` (enabled).

Today `POST /v1/mobile/order` answers `Unimplemented` for the operator `mci` (the table `orderOperators` in `src/entry/grpc/phone_order.go` has a nil entry, task 039); the MCI inquiry (task 036) already exists.

**Open points for the design step:** (1) the payment provider: Jiring Switch (`BillPay`, needs the adapter `Pay` and its mock), Parsian, or both by the provider pick of the seed; confirm with the operator which rows are enabled; (2) the bill types `mobile` and `mobilemidterm` in `billTypes` and in `PayRoutes` (Qabz has one inquiry bill type `mobile` that answers both terms; `bill` has two order bill types); (3) the price rule (direct price or the inquiry); (4) the key `settlement_inquiry_mci_mobile` (`0s`, not read); (5) the Postman request.

## Definition of Done

- `POST /v1/mobile/order` for the operator `mci` (`fullTerm` and `midTerm`) creates the order and the token as `bill`; the Irancell behavior of task 039 does not change.
- The chosen provider(s) pay `mobile` and `mobilemidterm`; `OrderComplete` answers `success`, `failed` and `pending`; the adapter `Pay` and its mock follow conventions § 15.

An order that it leaves `pending` or `in-progress` (a provider timeout, a short downtime) is finalized later by the status worker of that provider: the Parsian worker (task 045) with no code change; Jiring Switch has bill keys `get_status.*` and needs its own worker only if the design chooses that provider (operator decision).
- `docs/this_vs_bill.md` has an item for each difference from `bill`, with a `@todo` at the code; `system.html`, the config examples, the Postman collection, the CHANGELOG and the QA scenario in `OPEN_TASKS.md` are updated in the same commit.

## Design (implementer, 2026-11-16; waits for the operator approval)

Read on 2026-11-16 from `bill` commit `ff98e25` (not run) and from the Qabz source at `495f052`. D2 needs an operator decision; every other item follows `bill` and the tasks 044, 048, 052 and 053. Each difference from `bill` that a client, the payment service or the database can see gets an item in `docs/this_vs_bill.md` and a `@todo` at the code.

**Summary.** In `bill` the MCI order is the Irancell order (`Phone.NewOrderV2`, task 039) with two bill types: `fullTerm` is `mobile` (`BillTypeInquiryType` `final_term`), `midTerm` is `mobilemidterm` (`mid_term`). `NewOrder` (`service/billPayment/newOrder.go`) has no direct price rule for them (only `Irancell`), no provider fee (`AvailableBillTypes` has only the Karashop bill types), so the price is the raw inquiry when `billId` and `payId` are numeric, else the provider inquiry of the order bill type. The payment gateway (`app/paymentGateway.go`) maps both bill types to `JiringSwitchMCIMobile` and `ParsianMCIMobile`. No settlement.

### The order (`POST /v1/mobile/order`, operator `mci`)

- **D1. The entry.** `orderOperators` (`src/entry/grpc/phone_order.go`) gets the entry of `mci`: `midTerm` → `mobilemidterm` (new `provider.BillTypeMobileMidterm`), `fullTerm` → `mobile` (bill: `phoneBillTypeMapper`). The rest of `Phone.NewOrder` does not change: the request check, the phone in the 98 form as the inquiry ID, `orders.option` `mci`, the token with `order.PhoneData` (`service` `mci`). `rightel` stays `Unimplemented` (task 055). `POST /v1/mobile/complete-order` stays `Unimplemented`.
- **D2. The payment provider — OPERATOR DECISION.** bill pays both bill types through Jiring Switch (`BillPay`, seed rows 30 `mobile` and 29 `mobilemidterm`, both `disable`) or Parsian (`TopBill/Pay`, row 48 `mobile` `disable`, row 149 `mobilemidterm` `enable`). The provider row picks the gateway.
  - **(a) Parsian only (recommended)**, as water, electricity and gas. Only `parsian` gets the `PayRoutes` entries; an enabled Jiring Switch row gives the answer of a provider with no payment route (the case 2 `failed` answer of task 048). The Parsian status worker (task 045) finalizes a pending order with no code change.
  - **(b) Parsian and Jiring Switch.** Also: the Jiring adapter `Pay` (`InternalInquiryService/PayBill`; `provider_order_id` = the day of the year and the RRN, as text; the inquiry type) and its mock; a Jiring Switch status worker (`GetStatus`, the keys `providers.internal.jiring_switch.get_status.*`) on the shared status worker of task 050; a `jiring-switch` entry in the "already paid" windows (bill: `0` for both). This is about the size of task 050. If you choose (b), I propose a separate task for the Jiring Switch part, after task 054 with (a).
  - Note on the seed: with the seed rows, a `fullTerm` order has no enabled order provider (rows 30 and 48 are `disable`): HTTP 503, as bill. A `midTerm` order goes to Parsian (row 149). The stage and production rows can be different; please confirm them.
- **D3. The flow (`core/order`, two new `billTypes` entries).** `mobile`: `{inquiryType: final_term}`; `mobilemidterm`: `{inquiryBillType: mobile, inquiryType: mid_term}`.
  1. The order provider of (bill type, `order`).
  2. The "bill already paid" check (D4).
  3. The price: the raw inquiry when `billId` and `payId` are numeric (bill: `shouldInquiryWithPayID`); else (or when it fails) the provider inquiry of `mobile` with the phone, and the first inquiry with the inquiry type of the order bill type. **One difference, as the landline (item 42):** Qabz has one inquiry bill type `mobile` that answers both terms (task 036), so a `midTerm` order asks the inquiry providers of (`mobile`, `inquiry`) (seed: row 39 Parsian `enable`, row 28 Jiring Switch `disable`). bill asks the inquiry providers of (`mobilemidterm`, `inquiry`) (seed: row 27 Jiring Switch `enable`, row 41 Parsian `disable`). `this_vs_bill.md` item 42 is extended.
  4. A price of 0 is `FailedPrecondition` with bill's text; the provider fee is 0; the fee is `fee.Resolver` (default 9000; bill's `MobileFee` 0 in `GetBillMetaData` is not read by the fee, as bill); the `orders` row `initial`.
- **D4. The "bill already paid" window.** `providers.internal.parsian.settlement_inquiry_mci_mobile` exists, is `"0s"` and is not read. It is read for `mobile` and `mobilemidterm` of `parsian` (bill: `parsian.settlementInquiryMCIMobile`), default `0s` (bill's example value). **As bill, 0 is not "no check":** the entry is in the map, and the query has no time limit (bill: `GetLastStatusByBillIDPayID` with 0 minutes; Qabz `LastStatus` does the same). So a new MCI order is refused when the newest order of the same bill ID and pay ID, at any time, is `pending` or `success`. The config check rejects a negative value. The `config.yml.example` comment says this.
- **D5. Saved inquiries for an MCI order.** A new key `caching.paid_mobile`, default `1h` (bill: `caching.paidMobile: 1h`), for `mobile` only. `mobilemidterm` has no TTL in bill (`GetCachingBillTypeMap` of the order action), so a `midTerm` order always calls the provider (`NoCache`). They apply only when the price comes from the provider inquiry.
- **D6. The token `data` map.** `order.PhoneData` (the keys of bill's `NewOrderV2`, the same as Irancell). The metadata of both bill types (bill: `MciIcon`, `AboutIconMobileFinalTerm` / `AboutIconMobileMidterm`, `FeeDescriptionMobile`): `about` `قبض موبایل`, `aboutIcon` `https://public.780.ir/dl/Operator/logOhamrahaval2.svg`, `feeDescription` `کارمزد`.

### D2 revised — operator directive (2026-11-16)

Operator: "Make the full term to go to Parsian. For both mid and full, if Parsian is out of service (timeout, out of service etc.), then it goes to Jiring." This replaces D2 (a) and (b). It is a new behavior: `bill` has no payment fallback (one provider row, one gateway call).

- **D2a. The order provider.** Both terms are Parsian orders. The pick stays `PickProvider` (the first enabled row by `priority`, then `id`): Parsian rows have priority 9, Jiring Switch rows 10. The dev seed row 48 (`parsian`, `mobile`, `order`) changes to `enable` (Q4).
- **D2b. When the fallback runs (`order.Payments.Complete`, step 6).** The Parsian call is "out of service" when the request did not reach Parsian for sure: `providers.internal.parsian.enabled` false, no address, a refused connection, gRPC `Unavailable` before the call. A timeout is different: Parsian can have paid the bill (Q1).
- **D2c. The fallback rows.** After a Parsian "out of service", Qabz saves a new row `in-progress` with the Jiring Switch provider (`provider_id`, `provider_name` `jiring-switch`, the same RRN, the Parsian error as the message), calls Jiring Switch `PayBill`, and saves its answer as the final row. The status, metrics and span show both providers.
- **D2d. The status workers.** `pendingIDsSQL` takes the newest row of each order over all providers and then filters by the provider (today it filters first). Without this change, the Parsian worker sees the old Parsian `in-progress` row of a fallback order, asks Parsian, and can save a Parsian `failed` row over the Jiring `success` row. A new Jiring Switch status worker (`GetStatus`, the keys `providers.internal.jiring_switch.get_status.*`, on the shared worker of task 050) finalizes a Jiring `pending` order.
- **D2e. The Jiring adapter.** `Pay` (`InternalInquiryService/PayBill`; bill: `JiringSwitchMCIMobile`: `provider_order_id` = the day of the year and the RRN, as text; the inquiry type), `Status`, and their mocks (`debug.mocks.jiring_switch.pay_return_type`, `status_return_type`). The "already paid" windows get `jiring-switch`: `mobile` and `mobilemidterm` `0` (bill).
- **D2f. `this_vs_bill.md`.** A new item: the payment fallback from Parsian to Jiring Switch for the MCI bill types (bill has none), and the change to the pending query (item 43).

**Open questions (operator):**
- **Q1. A Parsian timeout.** The status is not known. Parsian can have paid the bill, so a payment through Jiring Switch at once can pay the MCI bill twice. Options: (a) ask Parsian the status (`TopBill/OrderInquiry`) once; go to Jiring Switch only when Parsian answers `failed`; `success` is success; else `pending` for the Parsian worker (recommended); (b) no fallback after a timeout: `pending`, as today; (c) go to Jiring Switch at once (the double payment risk).
- **Q2. Which provider is the fallback.** (a) The next enabled row of (bill type, `order`) after Parsian that has a payment route: the Jiring Switch rows 30 and 29 must be `enable` (with priority 10 Parsian stays first), and a disabled Jiring Switch row turns the fallback off (recommended); (b) always `jiring-switch` in the code, also when its row is `disable`.
- **Q3. The task size.** (a) One task 054 with all of it; (b) task 054 = both terms through Parsian (D1 to D12), and a new task for the fallback, the Jiring adapter `Pay` / `Status` and the Jiring Switch status worker (recommended: two smaller reviews and commits).
